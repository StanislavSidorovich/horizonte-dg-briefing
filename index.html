2 Case Context & Hard Constraints

Horizonte Municipality is a fictional Portuguese municipality (~142,000 residents, 5 planning zones, ~910 employees). Ahead of a Municipal Assembly hearing, two conflicting Q3 2026 numbers surfaced:

  • Operations (DASH-Q3): 82% of work orders (4,100 / 5,000) closed within 10 working days of field assignment. A draft press note shortened this to "82% of complaints resolved on time."
  • Citizen Services (CS-Q3): only 61% of incoming resident reports (2,440 / 4,000) received a final substantive reply within 10 working days of receipt.
  • The Trap: They measure different units and clocks across 6 disconnected systems (BairroDireto, Obras360, Terralink, GeoAssets, FinanceERP, ArquivoMunicipal) with no guaranteed end-to-end case_id.

4 The 4 Mandates (A–D) — Pick Our Preference

We need to rank our preferred mandate(s) in Moodle within 7 days of Session 1. Each mandate requires answering 3 specific questions:

A · Public Reporting ≤ €40k (90d)

Which service-performance number can we publish? Decide how to report 82% vs. 61% before the 19 Nov pre-hearing note.

  1. What to publish now (labels, populations, caveats) & what claims are unsubstantiated?
  2. Who owns definitions, validates evidence, produces & approves release? How are disputes resolved?
  3. Which 3 actions (90d & 6–12m) best improve reporting, and what proves they worked?
B · Systems Integration €265k / €55k PoC

One case, one digital journey: fund the integration? Vendor offer exceeds the €180k envelope and lacks a baseline or DPO sign-off.

  1. What can be approved now vs. gated (expenditure / data processing)?
  2. Which domains, mappings, definitions, owners & controls are prerequisites for a pilot?
  3. Which acceptance tests, fallbacks, owners & funding gates govern 90d & 6–12m?
C · Contractor Oversight €90k incentive

Can we certify contractor performance? Contractor reports 93% timeliness (744/800); targeted checks corroborate 72% (54/75). €25k audit option.

  1. What decision on verification, conditional approval, deferral or escalation, and by whom?
  2. Which indicators, source records, approvals & owners support reliable monitoring?
  3. What minimum independent checks, exception handling & evidence trail before next cycle?
D · Digital vs. Equity €160k AI vs €120k

Digital-first services, or service access for everyone? Competing €160k (+€55k/yr) AI assistant vs. €120k offline support pilot.

  1. What should be funded, piloted, deferred or rejected, and why (resource/inclusion trade-offs)?
  2. Who is accountable for definitions, accessibility, advice quality, personal data & outcomes?
  3. Which evidence & KPIs separate "digital activity" from "better, fairer service" before scale-up?
Recommended Mandate Priorities:
  • #1 Priority — Mandate A (Public Reporting): Purest Data Governance focus (Business Glossary, metric ownership, publication sign-off, evidence vs. assumptions). Realistic €40k 90-day scope, directly tackles the 82% vs. 61% core conflict, and is the cleanest to defend across all 6 Council roles.
  • #2 Priority — Mandate C (Contractor Oversight): Clear financial stakes (€90k incentive + €25k verification), a concrete data-quality trap (93% vs. 72%), and sharp governance boundaries between what the Council decides vs. Procurement/Finance. (B risks turning into an IT-procurement debate; D lacks baseline data and adds AI/ethics complexity.)
